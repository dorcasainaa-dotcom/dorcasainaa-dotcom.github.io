Analytical findings

Adult Social Care — primary pressure The largest single growth driver, rising from £95.3m to £131.8m (+38.3%) over four years. Driven by demographic demand growth and sustained National Living Wage increases on provider costs.
Housing — sharpest proportional rise Expenditure increased from £14.1m to £40.7m (+187.4%), reflecting significant temporary accommodation cost pressures and General Fund Housing demands.
Education — steady growth Increased from £196.3m to £238.9m (+21.7%), primarily driven by growing high needs funding requirements for pupils with Education, Health and Care Plans.
Highways & Transport — net income position Remains negative across all four years, reflecting TfL LIP grant income and parking revenues exceeding gross highway expenditure — normal for London Boroughs.
