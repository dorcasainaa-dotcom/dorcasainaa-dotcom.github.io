Love Island UK 2022 — Twitter Sentiment & NLP Analysis

Jan 2023

End-to-end NLP and sentiment analysis of 200,000 tweets scraped during the 2022 Love Island UK season using Python's snscrape library and the Twitter API. Applied a full preprocessing pipeline — tokenisation, stop word removal, lemmatisation, and regex hashtag extraction — before classifying tweet sentiment using TextBlob polarity scoring. Found that 69.44% of tweets were positive about the show, and that the most-tweeted couple did not win — suggesting Twitter engagement does not correlate with viewer votes. Visualised findings in Power BI covering sentiment distribution, hashtag frequency by couple, and geographic spread across 8 countries including the UK, USA, Nigeria, and Australia.

Local Authority Performance Dashboard — Microsoft Fabric

Apr 2026

Replication of the multi-directorate Power BI performance reporting I delivered at Richmond and Wandsworth Councils, rebuilt using DLUHC public Local Authority Revenue Outturn data across three financial years. The pipeline runs entirely in the browser via Microsoft Fabric: raw CSV files are uploaded to a Lakehouse Files zone, transformed and typed in Dataflow Gen2 using Power Query M, and written as Delta tables to the Lakehouse Tables zone. A Semantic Model defines a star schema — a central FACT_Expenditure table connected to Dim_Service, Dim_Date, and Dim_Geography via three single-directional many-to-one relationships — with eight DAX measures supporting KPI cards, year-on-year variance analysis, and People versus Place service category splits. The three-page report covers an executive KPI summary for DMT use, a service-level variance matrix with conditional formatting, and a multi-year trend analysis with average reference lines.
