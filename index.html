Love Island UK 2022 — Twitter Sentiment & NLP Analysis

Jan 2023

End-to-end NLP and sentiment analysis of 200,000 tweets scraped during the 2022 Love Island UK season using Python's snscrape library and the Twitter API. Applied a full preprocessing pipeline — tokenisation, stop word removal, lemmatisation, and regex hashtag extraction — before classifying tweet sentiment using TextBlob polarity scoring. Found that 69.44% of tweets were positive about the show, and that the most-tweeted couple did not win — suggesting Twitter engagement does not correlate with viewer votes. Visualised findings in Power BI covering sentiment distribution, hashtag frequency by couple, and geographic spread across 8 countries.
