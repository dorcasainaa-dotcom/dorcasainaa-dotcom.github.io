Wider Health Determinants Dashboard
Southampton & Statistical Neighbours

Multi-page Power BI dashboard analysing air pollution and child poverty across Southampton and three statistical neighbours (Portsmouth, Bristol, Plymouth) covering 2014–2023. Built on OHID public health data with custom DAX measures, England average benchmarks, RAG status indicators, and a bespoke background UI designed in Python.

Key findings

  • Air quality has improved across all four statistical neighbours since 2018. Portsmouth remains the worst performer, sitting above the England average (50th percentile) throughout the period. Plymouth consistently performs best.
  • Southampton's air pollution percentile score of 52 in 2022 places it above the England average, indicating worse-than-average air quality relative to all local authorities in England.
  • Child poverty rates have risen across all four cities since 2019, reversing the improving trend seen between 2014 and 2018. Southampton (18.1%) and Portsmouth (20.8%) both exceed the England average of 17%.
  • A correlation scatter chart confirms a positive relationship between air pollution and child poverty — areas with worse air quality also tend to have higher child poverty, suggesting the two determinants reinforce each other across this statistical neighbour group.
